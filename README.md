## JUS FEB ORG Management & Accounting System

A complete web and Android-based management system for family enterprise business operations, branches, shops, accounting, finance, members, products, documents, reports, and travel/transport services.

### Core Modules

- Owner & Admin Management
- Branch Management
- User Login & Access Control
- Member & Customer Management
- Accounting & Transactions
- Loan Management
- Interest Management
- Service Charge Management
- Payment & Due Management
- Cash In / Cash Out
- Shop Management
- Product Management
- Stock + / − Management
- Purchase & Sales
- Discount Management
- Documents & Digital Receipts
- Reports & Transaction History
- Travel & Transport Management
- Vehicle Management
- Passenger Management
- Online Booking
- Android APK Integration
