👥 Members

Member and customer information will be managed here. NID, name, mobile number, address and other information can be added in the next development stage.

🏢 Branch Management

Main Admin can create and manage branches. Each branch can have its own users and shops.

🏪 Shop Management

Shops can be managed under each branch. CCTV, Electronics, Electrical and other shops can be added.

👤 User Management

User roles include Main Owner, Main Admin, Branch Admin, Branch User and Shop User.

💰 Accounting

Original amount, service charge, interest, paid amount, due amount and total paid will be managed here.

💳 Loans & Finance

Loan records, repayment, interest, service charge and due information will be managed here.

📦 Products & Stock

Products, purchase, sales, stock quantity and product information will be managed here.

💵 Cash In / Cash Out

Cash income, cash expense and transaction records will be managed here.

📊 Reports

Branch reports, shop reports, accounting reports, loan reports and financial reports will be available here.

🚍 Transport

Transport and travel-related business information will be managed here.

⚙️ Settings

System settings, organization information, permissions and other configurations will be managed here.
